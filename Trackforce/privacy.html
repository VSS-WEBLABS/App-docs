Trackforce

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

The short version
  • Your money data lives on your phone, in an encrypted database. You don't need an account to use Trackforce.
  • Bank SMS and payment notifications are read on your phone. Their text never leaves it.
  • Data leaves your phone only for features listed below, most of which you choose to turn on.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers. We don't sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account, and everything we hold for it, at any time.

1. Who we are

Trackforce is made by AlphaDays ("we", "us"), based in India. For anything about this policy or your data, write to thealphadays@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Trackforce — accounts, entries, budgets, goals, investments, loans, people, notes and the documents and receipt photos you attach — is stored on your phone in an encrypted database. We don't receive it. The app lock (PIN or fingerprint) and its settings also stay on your phone.

3. SMS and notifications

If you allow it, Trackforce reads bank and card SMS (Android's SMS permissions) to turn transaction messages into drafts you confirm. If you choose notification capture instead, it reads notifications from the payment apps you pick.

  • Messages are read and understood on your phone. Their text is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Only messages from banks and payment services are used. One-time passwords, promotions and personal messages are ignored.
  • Once you've dealt with a message, its original text is kept on your phone for the period you choose (none, 30, 90 or 365 days), then deleted.
  • To read new bank formats, the app downloads updated message rules from our server. That's a download only — nothing about you is sent.

You can turn SMS or notification access off at any time in the app or in Android's settings.

4. Other permissions

  • Camera — to scan another phone's code when you connect them, and to photograph receipts. Photos stay on your phone.
  • Notifications — reminders about bills, budgets and drafts, created on your phone.
  • Display over other apps (optional) — the floating bubble for quick entries.
  • Fingerprint / face (optional) — unlocks the app; handled by Android, never seen by us.

About cloud backup

Your backup is encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM) before it's uploaded, with a random key created for your account. So that you can restore on a new phone, that key is stored with your account record. This protects the backup file if the file alone were ever exposed, but it is not "end-to-end" encryption that even we can't open. Only your latest backup is kept. Turning cloud backup off stops new backups; deleting your account deletes the backup.

About the services we use

Google (Firebase, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Google Play) and RevenueCat process this data for us, under their own privacy terms and security practices. As with any internet service, they and gold-api.com can see the IP address your request comes from.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or include advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own use.
  • We never ask for your bank login, card number or UPI PIN, and Trackforce can't move money.
  • We don't collect crash reports.

7. How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete it, erase the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Account record, subscription records and cloud backup: until you delete your account.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of purchases under its own policies, which we don't control.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in, your account record, your cloud backup, your subscription records and your RevenueCat customer record. The data on your phone isn't part of your account and stays until you erase it (Settings → Backup → Delete all data) or uninstall the app. If you can't use the app, see how to ask us to delete your account.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription — Google Play bills it. Cancel it in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, withdraw your consent by turning a feature or permission off, and raise a complaint. This includes your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to thealphadays@gmail.com from the email address of your account; we'll reply as soon as we can and within the time the law requires.

10. Security

Your data is encrypted on your phone, connections to our services use HTTPS, and access rules make sure each account can reach only its own records and backup. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and use the app lock.

11. Children

Trackforce is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its date. If a change affects how your data leaves your phone, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
