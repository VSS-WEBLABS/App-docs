Delete your Trackforce account

Trackforce · by AlphaDays

In the app

  1. Open Trackforce and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account (shown when you're signed in).
  3. Tap Delete account again, enter your PIN if the app lock is on, and confirm with Google.

It's done straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email thealphadays@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Trackforce account". We'll confirm it's your account and delete it within 30 days, and reply when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your Trackforce sign-in (the account itself)
  • Your account record: email, Premium status, last sign-in and backup dates, and your backup key
  • Your cloud backup
  • Your subscription records with us, and your customer record at RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us

What isn't

  • Data on your phone. It was never part of your account. Erase it in the app with Settings → Backup → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Google Play's purchase records. Google keeps these under its own policies.
  • An active Premium subscription. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it — Google Play bills it. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, before you delete your account.

Nothing else is kept: deletion is immediate and permanent, and your account can't be restored.
